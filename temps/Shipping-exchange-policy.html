SHIPPING 

We offer several domestic and international shipping options based on your budget and how urgently you want to improve your golf game.

Orders are fulfilled and shipped Monday through Friday within 24 hours of being placed. (Orders placed before 11 a.m. EST will be processed the same day. Orders placed after 11 a.m. EST will be processed the next business day.)

Our fulfillment center is closed on weekends and all major U.S. holidays. Please keep this in mind when selecting your shipping option.

DOMESTIC ORDERS

Blue Tees Golf offers free standard shipping on domestic orders of $50 or more. Expedited domestic shipping is also available.

- FREE standard shipping (3-10 business days) on orders $50 and up
- $11.99 standard shipping (3-10 business days) on orders under $50
- $24.99 2-day shipping (2 business days)
- $34.99 next day shipping (1-2 business days)

INTERNATIONAL ORDERS

International shipping prices are based on your location:

  • Canada and United Kingdom - $11.99 (arrives in 6-12 business days)
  • All other locations - $16.99 (arrives in 6-12 business days)

DELAYS IN SHIPPING

While we do our best to fulfill and ship your orders within 24 hours Monday through Friday, sometimes a delay occurs during shipping because of invalid shipping addresses, COVID restrictions, weather, or Customs.

If your package hasn’t arrived within the estimated timeframe, we suggest you:

  • Check the tracking information to see if it has been delivered. If it has been delivered and you didn’t receive it, check with your neighbors or mailroom (if you live in an apartment). Sometimes deliveries are mistakenly delivered to a neighbor or overlooked in a mailroom.
  • Contact your local post office for additional information on the shipment. Sometimes UPS and FedEx partner with the local postal service to make the final deliveries. In this case, your tracking information will indicate a transfer to the local post office.
  • If you still can’t find your delivery, please email our customer support team at support@blueteesgolf.com.

WEEKEND DELIVERY

Weekend delivery is not guaranteed with standard, 2-day, or next day shipping. Please keep this in mind when deciding on your shipping method.

Blue Tees Golf is not responsible for refunding 2-day and next day shipping for orders that do not arrive on the weekend.

RETURNS & EXCHANGES

We know you're going to love Blue Tees Golf products, but sometimes things don't work out. We get it. Before making a purchase, we encourage you to read the entire Blue Tees Golf Returns Policy below.

RETURN POLICY

Orders can be returned within 100 days from the date your order is delivered. To qualify for a full refund, returned items must be in their original condition with no evidence of wear-and-tear. All components included in the original order must also be returned. (For example, our rangefinder comes with a hardshell case, lanyard, carabiner, and battery cap.)

All returns are inspected upon receipt. This process may take 5-7 business days to complete. If your return does not pass our return inspection, you may be charged a 10% restocking fee or you may not qualify for a refund at all. Returned products that don't qualify for a refund will be recycled and cannot be returned to you.

For orders placed through our website, please follow the instructions below on how to return your product. We have partnered with Loop to make the process as easy as possible for our customers. We will provide a return shipping label* and the cost of the label will be deducted from your refund when we receive your package and your return is inspected.

*For international orders, you are responsible for shipping the returned item(s) back to us as we do not have the capacity to generate international return labels.

Once we’ve received and inspected your return, your refund will be processed and returned to the card used at purchase.

To initiate a Return or Exchange, please click “Get Started”.

EXCHANGES

We have a 2-year manufacturer’s warranty on all technology products. To qualify for an exchange, returned items must be in their original working condition with no evidence of wear-and-tear. All accessories included in the original order must also be returned.

We do not accept exchanges from second hand resellers like Ebay or Facebook Marketplace.

If purchased from our website, please follow the instructions in the section below to start the exchange process.

If purchased from an authorized reseller, you can submit a warranty claim directly through us. You must provide proof of purchase and are responsible for shipping the unit back to us Please send an email to support@blueteesgolf.com to start the exchange process..

All exchanges are inspected upon receipt. This process may take 5-7 business days to complete. If your returned rangefinder does not pass inspection, your exchange will be treated like a return.

If you want to upgrade your product after the 100-day return period has ended, you may benefit from our Trade Up Program. This program allows you to exchange your used product for a new one with discounted pricing. For more information, contact us at support@blueteesgolf.com.
